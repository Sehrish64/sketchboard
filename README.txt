Sketchboard (Excalidraw-style whiteboard)

1. Upload index.html to your hosting (GitHub Pages / Vercel / Netlify) or open it directly.
2. Share the page link: the #board-code at the end puts people on the same board.
3. Live collaboration uses Yjs + y-webrtc (peer to peer). To use your own signaling server, edit the SIGNALING line near the bottom of index.html.
